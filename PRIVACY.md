# Dusk — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 3, 2026*

Dusk is a screensaver for Roku devices. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

- **No network access.** Dusk makes no network requests. All of its images and code ship inside the package.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking.** Dusk contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- **Local settings only.** The only thing Dusk saves is your choice of scene and cycle interval. It is stored in your Roku device's local registry, never leaves the device, and is removed when you remove the screensaver.
- **No accounts and no purchases.**

Roku itself may collect information about your use of its devices and the Roku Channel Store under [Roku's privacy policy](https://docs.roku.com/published/userprivacypolicy/en/us). Dusk has no access to that information.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/timsaucer/dusk_roku_screensaver/issues.
